📜 Scoring system

Scoring is calculated per team for each completed match.

01 / KILL CONTRIBUTION

+0.05 Net Points

Each kill adds 0.05 net points to that team's total.

02 / ROUND DIFFERENCE

+0.25 Net Points

The selected match winner earns +0.25 net points per round of difference. The opponent receives no −0.25 round-difference points.

03 / PENALTIES

Deducted from Net Points

Enter a penalty as a positive number; it is subtracted from that team's net points.

04 / MATCH VICTORY

+5 Points

The selected match winner earns 5 points. The other team receives 0 points for that match.

Winner selection: The admin must choose the match winner explicitly. Round wins and kills are recorded separately; the winner is not automatically inferred from round wins.
